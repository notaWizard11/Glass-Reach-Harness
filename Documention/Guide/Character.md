# Character

Use **Character** to review your character's condition and belongings before deciding what to do.

## Sheet and growth

The sheet displays the character's description, health, water, reported conditions, faction and skills. **Growth & conditions** lists the progression marks and explanations recorded by the game. Expand **All reported character details** when you need the underlying fields.

The current screen uses the legacy **Body / Wits / Will** layout. The engine's newer twelve-attribute sheet system is not yet a dedicated visual sheet in the harness. An engine update or a prompt edit does not itself convert your character. Live observations remain the authority for the character's actual mechanics.

## Inventory

Click an item to open its description, contents where exposed, and available interactions. For carried items, **Personal inventory arrangement** lets you place it in **Ready / equipped visually** or **Backpack**.

That arrangement is saved locally. It does not equip, move or put away the item in the game. Use the item's actual game interactions or an **Act in game** request for those changes.

The load indicator reports bulk from the available data. **Known bulk** means some item sizes are missing, so the total is incomplete. Item counts and waterskin quantities reflect the last observation; use **Sync** if you need a fresh view.

For a quick check while playing, the [Play](Play.md) tab has collapsible stats and carried-item panels linking back here.
