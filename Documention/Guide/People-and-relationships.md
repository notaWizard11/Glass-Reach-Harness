# People & relationships

Use this tab to remember who your character knows and what the game has reported about their connections.

## People you know

Cards show known names, faction information and standing where available. Hover over a person for their last observed description, or click their name for more detail. On a touch device, opening the person's details is more useful than relying on hover.

Missing faction or standing information means it has not been established in the available record. A last-seen description is not a live guarantee that someone is still present or carrying the same things.

## Observed relationships

This section presents recorded connections and their direction. Pay attention to who holds the relationship toward whom: someone's standing toward you is not necessarily the same as yours toward them.

Technical report details are available from the small record control at the top right. It opens over the surrounding content so you can inspect the source without expanding the whole layout.

For a current spatial view, use [Atlas](Atlas.md). Visible characters with reported coordinates appear on the map; those without coordinates appear in Visible here. The atlas does not track last-seen positions.

## Use what you learn

Use **Inner thoughts** for a private question such as “What do I know about my relationship with the scout?” Switch to **Act in game** to actually address someone. Looking up a person here does not contact them or make them reachable from your current location.
