# Quests

Use **Quests** to keep track of promises, mysteries, errands and larger goals that emerge from the story.

## Find or add a quest

Choose **Discover quests from the story** to have Codex examine saved evidence for possible objectives. Discovered quests are suggestions, not commitments or actions taken in the world. The first discovery can require several model requests as it works through saved history; later runs continue from processed records.

Choose **Add quest** to write one yourself. You can also use **Inner thoughts**: “Add finding out what happened to Hollis to my quests.” Check the save confirmation after sending.

## Keep the list useful

Use **Edit** to change a quest's title, details or status. The **Show** filter lets you focus on open quests or a particular status:

| Status | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Discovered | A possibility to consider. |
| Active | Something you have decided to pursue. |
| Blocked | A goal waiting on information or another event. |
| Completed | An objective you consider finished. |
| Dismissed | A suggestion you do not want to pursue. |

Evidence references help you trace a quest back to the recorded story. Treat inferred objectives as suggestions to review. You can tell Inner thoughts to update a quest, such as “Mark the delivery quest completed.”

The quest list is local to the harness. Changing a status does not complete an engine objective or make your character act. The separate **In-game intentions** section shows game records; **Add intention in game** prepares a game operation for review.

To discover quests automatically, use **Watch & automation → While I'm elsewhere**. This uses Codex and does not authorize game actions.
